About Us

Welcome to Pickleball Gloucestershire — where great games, good vibes, and new friendships come together on and off the court. Whether you’re a seasoned player or picking up a paddle for the first time, our club is all about fun, community, and staying active in a relaxed, welcoming environment. Join us for friendly matches, social events, and plenty of laughs as we build a community that loves the game as much as the people who play it.
