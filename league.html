Second edition Wrap-Up

League Standings & Match Highlights

Division One

  • PFC OG Winners
  • Kitchen Nightmares Runners Up
  • Bombers A 3rd Place
  • Cotswolds Picklers A 4th Place
📝 Recap: PFC OG swept their semi-final 4-0 over Cotswolds Picklers A, then continued their dominant momentum through the final against Kitchen Nightmares to capture the title for the second consecutive year.

Division Two

  • Bristol Blues Winners
  • Swindon 419ers Runners Up
  • Dink Panthers Wild 3rd Place
  • Bombers B 4th Place
📝 Recap: Swindon 419ers fought through a brutal 5-game semi-final decider against Bombers B, but ran out of steam in the finals against Bristol Blues, who claimed the opening ladies' match to lock down Division Two.

Division Three

  • Bristol Dinkers Winners
  • Salty Picklers Runners Up
  • Manor Park 3rd Place
  • Cotswolds Picklers B 4th Place
📝 Recap: Bristol Dinkers locked in a tense, tight final round against the Salty Picklers—claiming the top division award with an authoritative 3-1 victory. Manor Park locked down 3rd place over Cotswolds B in a critical decider.

Division Four

  • Pirates Red Winners
  • David Lloyd Runners Up
  • Dink Panthers Mild 3rd Place
  • Avon 4th Place
  • Pirates Blue 5th Place
📝 Recap: Pirates Red hoisted their main sail and soared to victory in a thrilling format shift, rising dramatically from the bottom of the division all the way to the top spot!

League Wrap-Up & Celebrations

With the finals over, this concludes the second edition of the Gloucestershire Pickleball League. While enthusiasm and energy carried us through the games, the sheer strength and determination of competition kept us all battling until the absolute end. The growth and progress made by everyone over the weeks was a true pleasure to watch and highlights exactly why this league started in the first place—to bring clubs together in Gloucestershire, to play better pickleball, and to enjoy the games with unmatched team spirit!

The second edition won't be the last. We will be back bigger and stronger next time! The friendly but competitive atmosphere that defined our Sunday evenings will now be spent preparing and planning the next season, and we welcome even more people to get involved.
