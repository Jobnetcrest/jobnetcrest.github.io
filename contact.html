Frequently Asked Questions

Yes, player insurance is mandatory. For safety and liability purposes, all players must be registered members of Pickleball Gloucestershire via Pickleball England before stepping onto the court. You can easily complete your registration online through our official Club Link.

We have vibrant online spaces to keep you connected! You can join our active WhatsApp Group to chat with players, coordinate games, and receive real-time updates regarding court changes or weather cancellations. You can also join our community on Facebook to catch up on club news and photos. Contact an organizer at a session or message us using the contact links to get direct access to our communities!

No equipment required to start! We provide club paddles and high-quality pickleballs for all players during our standard club sessions. If you choose to invest in your own paddle later, our organizers are happy to give you gear recommendations.

Absolutely! Pickleball is incredibly beginner-friendly and easy to pick up. We regularly welcome new players and offer basic introductory coaching during our sessions to get you serving, rallying, and scoring in no time.

Once you have joined the club, you will be given access to PBTable where you will be able to book and pay for your sessions. Please note that a session cannot be booked if there is no money available in your wallet, so make sure your account is topped up before trying to reserve a court slot.

Indoor sessions at venues like Oxstalls Tennis Centre always run regardless of the weather. For outdoor recreational sessions, we monitor weather updates closely. If a session is cancelled due to rain, all registered players will be notified immediately via our WhatsApp group.
