ABOUT US

WHO WE ARE

Our Mission: To grow the game of pickleball across Gloucestershire.

Our Community: Players of all ages, from total beginners to match-ready competitors.

Our Vibe: Inclusive, social, active, and welcoming.

WHAT WE DO

Social Play: Weekly club sessions for casual rallies and fun matches.

Coaching: Beginner-friendly "Intro to Pickleball" and improver clinics.

Local Leagues: Competitive match play through community hubs like the Oxstalls Sports Park in Gloucester.

WHY PLAY WITH US?

Easy to Learn: Simple rules mean you can start playing on day one.

Good for Health: A fantastic, low-impact workout that is gentle on joints.

Great Friends: Meet local people who share a passion for sport and fun.
