Festival Format

Dink & Link 🔗

The Festival Format

No tournament pressure, no fixed partners—just a mix of open-court play, social networking, coaching, and gear testing.

The Social Mixer

Show up solo or with friends. You will rotate partners and opponents after every game, meeting new players on every court.

The Rotation Station

Step off the court, slide your personalized magnetic name tag onto the central queue board, and head straight to the hydration station and some refuelling before the next pairing.

Play to 11

Quick, fast-paced games to 11 points (win by 2) keep the energy high and ensure maximum court time for everyone.

Curated Skill Courts

Courts are organized by experience level so you always get a fun, balanced game before winding down at the grazing tables.

Meet. Dink. Paddle Tap. Rotate. Repeat!

Important Booking Window info

Slots are bookable through Freedom Leisure exactly 2 weeks prior to the event date. These sessions will be utilizing additional dedicated courts separate from the main festival play.

Available Session Slots
  • Morning Block: 10:00am – 11:00am
  • Midday Block: 12:00pm – 1:00pm
  • Afternoon Block: 2:00pm – 3:00pm
Core Syllabus Covered

Every individual slot covers continental paddle grips, legal underhand serving rules, baseline groundstroke depth, and an introduction to Non-Volley Zone boundaries.

Max 12 players per session slot. All three session schedules cover identical content.

Getting There & Parking

The Venue

Oxstalls Sports Park

Plock Court, Tewkesbury Road, Longford, Gloucester, GL2 9DW.

Main Car Park

Free parking is available on-site for up to 130 cars. Please note spaces fill up quickly during major tournaments and busy weekend festival slots.

Accessible Parking

Designated bays for Blue Badge holders are located directly on the tarmac near the main entrance infrastructure and central changing facilities.

Overflow Options

University of Gloucestershire

Oxstalls Campus, Gloucester, GL2 9HW.

Campus Overflow Parking

If the main sports facility car park reaches capacity, peak event overflow spacing is accessible at the adjacent University campus. It is a scenic, flat 10-minute walk back to the court arena. Parking charges apply

Public Transport & Active Travel

The distance from Gloucester Station/Gloucester Transport hub to Plock Court is roughly 1.5 miles and takes about 30 minutes on foot if you prefer a direct walk via Estcourt Road or Tewkesbury Road
